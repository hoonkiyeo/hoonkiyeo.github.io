About me

I earned my B.S. in Data Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. After graduation, I returned to South Korea and worked as a data scientist in Seoul, developing forecasting and recommendation systems.

I am now pursuing an M.S. in Operations Research at the Georgia Institute of Technology. I work with Prof. Sen Na on applying derivative-free stochastic optimization to a constrained learning problem, connecting the algorithm’s formulation with implementation and computational experiments.

Research interests

I am interested in optimization algorithms, particularly for problems involving uncertainty, and their applications to learning and decision-making. This includes optimization for machine learning as well as optimization for operational decision-making.

In a Ph.D., I hope to study and develop optimization methods, with an emphasis on understanding what makes them effective in real-world applications.

With Prof. Sen NaWork in progress

Derivative-free optimization for constrained learning

How can we optimize a learning model when evaluations are noisy and costly, and its parameters must satisfy an equality constraint?

I study this question by implementing and evaluating derivative-free stochastic SQP (DF-SSQP) for a single-index learning problem. This ongoing work examines how the method performs in a learning application and how its algorithmic choices affect optimization.
