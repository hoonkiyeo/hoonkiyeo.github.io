Research

Research

My research interests are in stochastic and derivative-free optimization. My current work studies derivative-free stochastic optimization for equality-constrained learning problems.

Current Directions
  • Constrained stochastic and derivative-free optimization. Methods for stochastic black-box problems with deterministic equality constraints and noisy function-value feedback, including objectives defined through inner training and validation procedures.
  • Optimization with learning-defined objectives. Problems in which each candidate requires finite stochastic model training, with validation loss providing noisy feedback for the outer optimization method.
