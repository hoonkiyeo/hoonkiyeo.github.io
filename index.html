Research

Interests

Research interests

  • Optimization for machine learning — applying constrained stochastic optimization methods to learning problems.
  • Statistical inference for optimization — quantifying uncertainty in the optimal solution of an optimization problem, such as confidence statements about optimal portfolio weights.
  • Generative models for decision-making — diffusion and generative methods for modeling uncertainty in structured optimization problems.

Statistical Inference for Equality-Constrained Portfolio Optimization via Derivative-Free SQP
Work in progress · target: NeurIPS 2026 Workshop — with Sen Na
