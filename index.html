About

At Georgia Tech, I am working with Prof. Sen Na on derivative-free stochastic optimization for equality-constrained learning problems. My current project studies whether derivative-free stochastic SQP can optimize a single-index direction when each objective evaluation requires stochastic neural-network training and returns only noisy scalar validation feedback.

As a real-data testbed, the project uses past industry-level risk features to predict future benchmark risk through a chronological train, validation, and test pipeline. In parallel, I began an independent study of diffusion-based methods for offline optimization in June 2026. Before Georgia Tech, I worked as a data scientist in Seoul, building forecasting and recommendation systems in production. I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

Research

Research

I am broadly interested in optimization under uncertainty and the ways learned models can support optimization. My current work focuses on constrained stochastic black-box problems and diffusion-based offline optimization.

Current Directions
  • Constrained stochastic and derivative-free optimization. Methods for stochastic black-box problems with deterministic equality constraints and noisy function-value feedback, including objectives defined through inner training and validation procedures.
  • Learning-based methods for black-box optimization. Neural and generative models that support evaluation or search when direct objective access is limited or only offline data are available.
