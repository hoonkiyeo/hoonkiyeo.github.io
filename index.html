About

My research is on black-box, stochastic optimization under equality constraints, where part of the objective is parameterized by a neural network.

Before Georgia Tech, I spent over two years as a data scientist in Seoul, building forecasting and recommendation systems in production. That applied work is what pulled me toward the methods underneath — why these models work, and when their outputs can be trusted. I am advised by Prof. Sen Na and am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

Research

Research

I'm interested in optimization methods, the statistical reliability of the solutions they produce, and generative models for decision-making under uncertainty.

Interests
  • Stochastic & derivative-free optimization. Applying these methods to learning problems.
  • Statistical inference for optimization. Quantifying uncertainty in the optimal solution of an optimization problem, such as confidence statements about optimal portfolio weights.
  • Generative models for decision-making. Diffusion and generative methods for modeling uncertainty in structured optimization problems.
Projects
Statistical Inference for Equality-Constrained Portfolio Optimization via Derivative-Free SQP
Work in progress · with Sen Na

Applying a derivative-free stochastic SQP method to long-short portfolio optimization under an equality (budget) constraint, with asymptotic-normality inference on the optimal portfolio weights as the central contribution.
