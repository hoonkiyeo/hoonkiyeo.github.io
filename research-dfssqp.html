Current research project

Equality-Constrained Single-Index Learning via DF-SSQP

A methodological study of derivative-free stochastic SQP for equality-constrained learning problems in which each objective evaluation requires stochastic inner training and yields only noisy scalar validation feedback.

Work in progress with Prof. Sen Na Georgia Tech ISyE stochastic black-box optimization
Overview

Optimization methodology first

The outer variable w ∈ ℝ48 is a shared single-index direction. For each candidate, a fresh neural prediction model is fitted using a fixed finite-step stochastic procedure. Its validation loss supplies noisy scalar feedback to the outer optimizer. The unit-norm equality constraint fixes the index scale, with direction recovery evaluated up to sign.

ROLE

The study progresses from known-solution optimizer validation to synthetic learning experiments, followed by a planned industry-risk forecasting application. Current work is at the synthetic experiment-design stage.

Questions

Two levels of inquiry

Primary · Methodological

Can DF-SSQP optimize an equality-constrained direction when every objective evaluation requires stochastic inner training?

The planned synthetic experiments examine how constraint-Jacobian information affects this task by pairing an analytic-constraint variant (AI) with the fully zeroth-order mode (ZI). Both use noisy scalar objective feedback and will be reported separately.

Secondary · Application

Can the learned index recover a planted signal and add predictive information beyond the control?

Synthetic experiments separate recovery of the generating direction, up to sign, from improvement in validation loss. Future benchmark-risk forecasting is the planned application; predictive gains have not yet been established.

Formulation

A stochastic learning objective

The inner model maps the industry-risk vector to a scalar index, while the outer problem selects the index direction from noisy validation feedback.

Inner prediction model
ŷm = βum + Fθ(w⊤zm)

The neural link receives a scalar index of the features z; β accounts for the control u. For each w, β and θ are finite-training outputs that depend on the training randomness ξ, rather than exact inner minimizers.

Outer optimization problem
minw ∈ ℝ⁴⁸ Eξ[L(w, ξ)]   s.t.   w⊤w − 1 = 0

The expectation is over inner-training randomness with the data and training procedure fixed. A known synthetic generating direction does not make the minimizer of this finite-training objective known.

Optimization loop

From paired evaluations to an update

Each objective call fits a fresh model on the same fixed training data and returns validation loss. Paired plus/minus evaluations share initialization and minibatch randomness. Perturbed candidates are evaluated without projection onto the sphere.

One paired objective estimate · Two fresh inner fits
  1. 01

    Perturb direction

    Evaluate w + bΔ and w − bΔ without projection.

  2. 02

    Fit two models

    Fresh training with shared initialization and minibatch randomness ξ.

  3. 03

    Evaluate losses

    Return a scalar validation loss for each candidate on fixed data.

  4. 04

    Update direction

    Use the paired estimate in the equality-constrained SQP step.

Constraint information

w⊤w = 1 · Analytic Jacobian (AI) or zeroth-order estimate (ZI)

Stage A validation · Technical details

The frozen confirmation missed its predeclared zero-failure criterion: 431 of 432 path gates and 44 of 45 group gates passed. Stage A closed with this qualification after the implementation was judged sufficiently validated to proceed.

The retained exception was traced to early zeroth-order constraint-Jacobian estimation followed by persistent step-size contraction. The failure remains part of the record; these experiments do not establish predictive gains in the learning application.
